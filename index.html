How an estimate works

From damage to done, in four steps

  1. 01

    Send photos

    All sides of the damage, plus the VIN plate. Use the online form or call us.

  2. 02

    Get a written estimate

    The estimate costs $350, and that amount is credited toward the repair if we do the work.

  3. 03

    Parts & schedule

    We order the parts. Work starts once all required parts are received, on a schedule agreed with you.

  4. 04

    Repair & pick-up

    Your truck is repaired and ready to get back on the road.

Questions

Before you call

How much does an estimate cost?

A written estimate costs $350. That amount is credited toward the repair if we do the work.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the damage and the parts needed. Work starts once all required parts are received, and we agree the schedule with you for each project.

Do you work with my insurance company?

We work with all insurance companies and independent adjusters. It's your choice which shop repairs your truck.

Which trucks do you repair?

Freightliner, Volvo, Kenworth, Peterbilt, Mack and other heavy-duty makes.

Do you do mechanical repair, towing or DOT inspections?

No. We do collision and body work on heavy-duty trucks only — no mechanical repair, towing, DOT inspections, welding or frame straightening, and no trailers or box trucks.

Can I bring my own parts?

No. We don't accept customer-supplied parts.

How does payment work on a large repair?

Large repairs require insurance proceeds or an agreed advance payment for parts and work. We agree payment arrangements for each project.

Can I just drop my truck off?

Please call at least 2 hours before you arrive so we can coordinate drop-off and parking.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Russian and Lithuanian.
