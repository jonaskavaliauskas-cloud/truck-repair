Process

How an estimate works

  1. Send photos

    All sides of the damage, plus the VIN plate. Use the online form or call us.

  2. Written estimate

    The estimate costs $350, and that amount is credited toward the repair if we do the work.

  3. Parts & schedule

    We order the parts. Work starts once all required parts are received, on a schedule agreed with you.

  4. Repair & pick-up

    Your truck is repaired and ready to get back on the road.

About HENSA

Quality. Precision. Reliability.

Founded in 2022 by Svajunas Jocius, HENSA Trucks & Body Repair Corp. provides heavy-duty truck collision and body repair in Naperville, Illinois. We serve owner-operators, trucking companies and commercial fleets throughout Chicagoland, with a focus on quality workmanship, precision, honest communication and customer satisfaction.
